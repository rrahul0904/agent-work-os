What makes the platform different

Not a single LLM call

Planner, researcher, critic, semantic compiler, renderer and reviewers have separate responsibilities. Architecture and presentation quality are checked before export.

Editable by design

The owned semantic layer targets DiagramSpec, DeckPlan and layered creative objects so exports can remain editable instead of becoming screenshot-only artifacts.

Agents everywhere

One hosted service is exposed through web, API, MCP and thin skills/plugins for Codex, Claude, Cursor, Antigravity, Gemini and compatible clients.

Foundations

Small concepts that improve reasoning quality.

Core

History as hypothesis shaping

Learn to ask questions because they change probabilities, safety, or the next information request—not because they are on a memorized list.

Safety

Red flags before detail

Practice identifying when a learning case should stop normal reasoning and prioritize urgent escalation concepts.

Reasoning

Differential ranking

Rank hypotheses using fit, contradictions and consequences of being wrong.

Reflection

Commit, then compare

Make your working hypothesis visible before feedback. This reduces hindsight bias and makes practice measurable.

# Selling Channels for n8n Products

## Channel strategy

Use multiple channels with different jobs instead of relying on one marketplace.

### 1. n8n Creator Hub — discovery + credibility
Best for native n8n templates. Publish free templates first, reach verified-creator status, then submit paid templates. Use simpler products for discovery and flagship products for paid conversion.

### 2. Lemon Squeezy — primary owned storefront
Best default checkout for commercial workflow kits, bundles and software-like digital products. Merchant-of-record model reduces sales-tax/VAT operational burden. Use direct storefront sales for flagship products and bundles; marketplace listing is optional and should be evaluated against its higher marketplace fee.

### 3. Gumroad — secondary creator storefront
Useful for quick digital downloads, bundles, tutorials and services. Good for audience-driven sales and free/pay-what-you-want lead magnets. Higher direct-sale fee than Lemon Squeezy should be reflected in pricing.

### 4. Whop — community/subscription channel
Best for recurring access: monthly workflow drops, support community, template vault, updates and implementation membership. Also suitable for one-time products.

### 5. Own website + Stripe/Lemon Squeezy checkout — long-term brand
Create a branded automation store with SEO landing pages for each outcome/vertical. The website owns the customer journey while checkout is delegated to a payment provider.

### 6. Services channels
Sell implementation/customization separately from templates through direct inbound, LinkedIn content, Upwork/Contra where appropriate, and product checkout service SKUs.

## Recommended launch order

1. n8n Creator Hub: publish 3 high-quality free templates to earn verified status.
2. Lemon Squeezy: launch flagship paid catalog and bundles.
3. Gumroad: mirror selected creator-oriented products and lead magnets.
4. Whop: launch `n8n Automation Vault` membership after the catalog reaches 10+ verified SKUs.
5. Branded store: make it the canonical catalog and route marketplace traffic back to product documentation/support.

## Product-channel mapping

| Product type | Primary | Secondary |
|---|---|---|
| Native workflow template | n8n Creator Hub | Gumroad |
| Premium workflow kit | Lemon Squeezy | Gumroad |
| Vertical business automation bundle | Own store / Lemon Squeezy | Whop |
| Developer/reliability pack | Lemon Squeezy | n8n Creator Hub |
| Membership/template vault | Whop | Own store |
| Installation/customization | Own store | Gumroad service/direct sales |

## Initial free templates for Creator verification

Choose independently authored/clean-room templates that are useful but do not give away the flagship product:

1. Production-safe webhook retry + DLQ starter
2. Generic lead qualification + human approval starter
3. Generic meeting-summary + task-extraction starter

After these are approved, submit paid versions/bundles where the Creator Hub rules permit.
